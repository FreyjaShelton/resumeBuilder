Resume Tailor

Paste your resume and a job listing, then either append the listing as-is or have Claude tailor your resume to it.

Only needed for AI tailoring, not for the plain append. Your key is stored solely in this browser's localStorage and is sent directly from your browser to Anthropic's API — it never touches any server of ours. Get a key at console.anthropic.com/settings/keys.

"Export as PDF" opens your browser's print dialog with a formatted one-page version of the result above — choose "Save as PDF" as the destination. Everything in it is plain, visible text; nothing is hidden.

Runs entirely in your browser. No backend, no data collection.
